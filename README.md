## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores a **Video Game Sales dataset** to understand trends in the gaming industry across different regions, platforms, genres, and publishers.

The goal is to perform **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** and extract meaningful insights using Python data analysis and visualization libraries.

---

## 📊 Dataset Information

The dataset includes information such as:

- 🎮 Game Name  
- 🕹️ Platform (PS4, Xbox, PC, etc.)  
- 📅 Year of Release  
- 🎯 Genre  
- 🏢 Publisher  
- 🌎 Sales Data:
  - North America (NA_Sales)
  - Europe (EU_Sales)
  - Japan (JP_Sales)
  - Other Regions
  - Global Sales

## 📸 Visualizations

The following visualizations were created during the analysis to explore patterns in the dataset:

### 📊 Bar Charts
- Top platforms by total sales  
- Most popular genres  
- Leading publishers in the gaming industry  

### 📉 Histograms
- Distribution of global sales  
- Frequency of game releases across different ranges  

### 📦 Box Plots
- Detection of outliers in sales data  
- Comparison of sales spread across genres and platforms  

### 🔥 Heatmap
- Correlation matrix of numerical features (NA, EU, JP, Global Sales)  

### 📈 Line Plots
- Year-wise trend of video game sales  
- Market growth and decline patterns over time  

### 🥧 Pie Charts
- Regional sales distribution (NA vs EU vs JP vs Other)  
- Market share breakdown by region or platform  

---

## 📌 Key Insights

- 🎮 Action and Sports genres dominate the market  
- 🕹️ Certain platforms consistently outperform others in global sales  
- 🌎 North America leads in total video game sales  
- 🏢 A few publishers control a large share of the industry  
- 📉 Sales trends vary significantly across regions and time periods
